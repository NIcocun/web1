How my reading changed

books and pages since 2023, from my Goodreads.

Before 2023

How it started

As a kid, I listened to a few audiobooks, like Can't Hurt Me. But I only really started reading when someone I was working with on a project told me to read Rick Rubin's The Creative Act first. I never stopped.

2023

The self-help year

34 books. 21 of them were self-help and philosophy.

“I paused and rewinded this book over 50 times to write down what he was saying.”my review of The Almanack of Naval Ravikant

2024

135 books, and a lot of Osho

My biggest year, about two books a week: spirituality, the Stoics, and every Osho book I could find.

“Ever since I started reading this book, I've been experimenting with the principles, and I ended up living my dream.”my review of Reality Transurfing

Late 2024

Reading on the road

Project Goat started, and the books came with me.

“I have wanted to read this book since I first watched Into the Wild and started thinking about Project Goat.”my review of Education of a Wandering Man

2025

Tired of self-help, so fiction took over

In January I picked up Harry Potter because I was tired of self-help. Fiction went from 11 of my 135 books in 2024 to 40 of 93 in 2025.

“I read it because I was tired of self-help books, and I'm so glad I read it.”my review of Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone

2026 and now

What I'm reading now

Lately: history, geopolitics, physics, Steve Jobs again, yoga texts, and 1,300-page fantasy epics. Three of these I'm in the middle of right now.

That's the story so far.

Every one of these books is waiting for you back in the library.
