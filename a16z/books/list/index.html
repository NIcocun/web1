Words of Radiance (The Stormlight Archive, #2)

Brandon Sanderson

★★★★★ · Read October 22, 2025 · favorite

This is the highest rated book on GoodReads, and it completely lives up to that expectation. I listened to the whole book because I'm a good Vorin man (lol). The Sanderlanche hit while I was walking on the road/sidewalk and it had me jumping around and punching the air, and if a book has me doing that in public then it did something right. The whole book had me in a trance from the very the prologue’s very first word. For 14 days, I didn't want to do ANYTHING but read this book. It was on my mind 24/7. To the point that it had me repeating Kaladin's ideals while paddling out to catch a wave during a surfing session because the waves kept falling on me hahahaha.

“Honor is dead, I’ll see what I can do” had tears forming in my eyes. One of the best things I’ve read along with when he bonds with Syl again

This book is Shallan's book, we see her flashbacks instead of any other characters (in The Way of Kings it was Kaladin's flashbacks) and I loved her as a character way more than the first book. She has real stakes and her flashbacks reveal many of her past actions and why the people around her did the things they did (iykyk). I also thought she was more likeable in general, the fact that she was forced to grow as a person because of Jasnah’s death definitely had a contributing factor to that. In book 1, everytime a Shallan chapter was next I groaned and really wanted to skip it (up until the end), and this book had me excited for Shallan's chapters and had me wondering what she was doing whenever we were looking at another POV. Adolin is another character that comes into the spotlight in this book for me. In the first book, he just felt like Dalinar's son. But in Words of Radiance he's more than that. And Kaladin... is one of my favorite character in the whole series (Wit, lopen, and dalinar are close as well), and probably of fiction in general. Kaladin's arc in this book is incredible. From discovering his powers and beating the shit out everyone in the 4v1 and the Szeth 1v3, to almost killing Syl (adhd tinkerbelle) and losing his powers, to regaining the powers because he finally has the balls to do/see what is "right" in his eyes, regaining the bond with Syl, and flying (or falling lol) into the center of the shatterplains and basically saving the day and killing Szeth. I have no clue what is going to happen in the future books, and I’m excited to see what is made of these characters.

This is a book that automatically enters my favorite list, and it got a 5 stars in my eyes way before it ended. I love Sanderson's prose, dialogue, action scenes, the cliffhangers at the end of each part and POV change, the worldbuilding (even though the interludes sometimes seem useless, they add soo much to the world because you feel like things other than what the MCs are doing are going on, the mythology, religions, and creatures make the scale of the world huge), the magic system is easy to understand with clear rules set in place, he's great at foreshadowing events (for example, when the stormfather said one of her daughters was disobeying him, I instantly knew it was Syl bonding with Kaladin again, the stakes are real, and the characters feel real because they aren’t perfect.

I wish this 1000+ page book and 50 hour audiobook was longer.

It’s a great book that goes perfectly with The Anxious Generation by Jonathan Haidt. It has a whole chapter dedicated to the transition of play-based childhood to phone-based and its effects. Both of these books are must-reads in my opinion. This book will help you understand a lot of things from this subject, not only why we are losing attentions but how and why they are doing it.
